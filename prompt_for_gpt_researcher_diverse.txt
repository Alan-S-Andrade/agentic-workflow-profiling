Investigate the sudden collapse of a hypothetical online marketplace's seller
fulfillment rate from 96% to 61% over six weeks. Treat this as an incident
investigation, not a general explainer.

Build and test at least five competing hypotheses spanning: warehouse capacity,
carrier/network disruption, fraud-policy changes, payment holds, demand spikes,
and seller-software outages. For every hypothesis, gather independent evidence
from primary sources, industry reporting, operational metrics guidance, and
postmortem-style case studies. Separate observed facts from inferences and
explicitly identify contradictory evidence.

Create a dated event timeline, a causal-factor matrix (hypothesis, supporting
evidence, counterevidence, confidence, and leading indicators), and a
prioritized 30/60/90-day remediation plan. Before writing the final report,
perform a dedicated gap-analysis pass: list the three most consequential
unknowns, explain how each could change the conclusion, and propose the next
data collection step for each. Include citations throughout.
